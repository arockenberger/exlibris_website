# Norwegian Exlibris. Catalogue of 8650 Norwegian Exlibris

## Source

This table is the fully digital version of the *Norwegian Exlibris Catalogue* as it was compiled by Thor Bjørn Schyberg and published in Frederikshavn 1974 with *Exlibristen* publishing house c/o Klaus Rödel.

*Norske Exlibris. Fortegnelse over 8650 norske exlibris. Utgitt ved Thor Bjørn Schyberg. Frederikshavn, Exlibristen, 1974.*

A [scanned version](http://urn.nb.no/URN:NBN:no-nb_digibok_2013042408097) of the printed book is held at the Norwegian National Library.

The catalogue was printed in only 100 hard copies and has long been out of print and almost impossible to get a hold of, even via the antiquarian book market. 

The copyright holder, Klaus Rödel, has granted Annika Rockenberger permission to publish the catalogue in this form.

## Digitisation

Annika Rockenberger digitised the catalogue based on the copy in Rolf Nysted Heier's collection, which she purchased in 2024. 

The catalogue was scanned, processed with automatic text recognition, transformed into a table, and carefully proofread, however, it may still contain factual errors and typos. Handle the information accordingly.

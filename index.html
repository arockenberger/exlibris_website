Norske exlibris: fortegnelse over 8650 norske exlibris

Source

This table is the fully digital version of the Norwegian Exlibris Catalogue as it was compiled by Thor Bjørn Schyberg and published in Frederikshavn 1974 with Exlibristen publishing house c/o Klaus Rödel. The catalogue was printed in only 100 hard copies and has long been out of print and almost impossible to get a hold of, even via the antiquarian book market. The copyright holder Klaus Rödel has granted Annika Rockenberger permission to publish the catalogue in this form.

Digitisation

Annika Rockenberger digitised the catalogue based on the copy in Rolf Nysted Heier's collection, which she purchased in 2024. The catalogue was digitised, processed with automatic text recognition, transformed into a table, and carefully proofread, but may still contain factual errors and typos. Handle the information accordingly.
